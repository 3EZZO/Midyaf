The Problem

SAR 12B Market. Zero Unified Platform.

A government ministry hosts 500 VIP delegates for a Riyadh summit. Coordination happens across 47 WhatsApp groups. Three diplomatic cars are sent to the wrong terminal. The minister's aide waits 40 minutes at the airport. The organizing company loses SAR 200K in overtime and is never rehired.
01 70% of event coordination still happens via WhatsApp with no tracking, no analytics, no accountability.
02 Average 30% cost overrun on hospitality logistics per event — hidden in manual vendor management and overtime.
