# Midyaf: Project Context

## Product

Midyaf is a Saudi-focused platform for hospitality and logistics operations at events. It replaces fragmented WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, calls, and manual coordination with one controlled system for planning, procurement, guest movement, field execution, reporting, and operational oversight.

The platform serves event organizers and their clients, suppliers, logistics teams, guests, captains/drivers, and field coordinators. It should be Arabic-first while also serving international guests and workforce users.

## Primary outcome

Operate guest hospitality and transportation with a single source of truth, clear ownership and approvals, controlled supplier quotations, reliable arrival/movement/departure workflows, live visibility for authorized teams, and auditable financial and operational actions.

## Users

| User / interface | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Midyaf executive administration | Cross-platform financial, operational, technical, and service oversight |
| Organizing company | Creates events, assigns leadership, purchases services, approves decisions |
| Supplier | Receives RFQs, submits quotations, executes contracted services |
| Client | Views approved progress, submits permitted requests, receives approved reports |
| Event management / logistics manager | Runs live operations, teams, tasks, schedules, and reports |
| Guest | Views itinerary and manages authorized arrival/departure/transport interactions |
| Captain / driver | Executes journeys, updates status, follows shifts and tasks |
| Field coordinator | Handles airport, hotel, and venue tasks, handovers, support |
| Radar | Restricted live operations view |

## Operating principles

- Every user sees only authorized tenant, event, role, and data scope.
- The logistics manager is the operational controller for an event.
- Every task has owner, status, deadline, feedback, and history.
- Material changes notify relevant users and require acknowledgement.
- Critical actions require server-side authorization and audit records.
- Confidential guests are code-only unless an approved elevated reveal process is met.
- Human approval is required for financial commitments, sensitive-data disclosure, and exceptional operational decisions.

## Current objective

Audit the existing deployed application against baseline requirements and founder feedback before changing code.
