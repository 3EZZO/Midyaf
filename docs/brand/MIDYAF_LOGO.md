# MIDYAF (مضياف) — Brand Identity Assets

## Concept Rationale
The emblem refines Midyaf's heritage hospitality canopy into an architectural Saudi reception pavilion (*khaymah*) embracing a pointed Najdi palace archway (*qasr*) and a warm central hospitality hearth, crowned by a faceted Sadu diamond finial and anchored on a welcoming champagne-brass horizon arc. Every element uses bold, balanced negative space so the mark stands alone cleanly from `16px` favicons up to `1024px` hero lockups on Qasr Obsidian basalt or warm ivory surfaces.

## Typography & Licences
All wordmarks in the SVG masters are converted to pure vector `<path>` outlines (zero embedded fonts, zero `<text>` elements, zero embedded rasters):
- **Arabic (`مضياف`)**: **Noto Kufi Arabic** (Weight 700 Bold) — **SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1)**, extracted directly from `@fontsource/noto-kufi-arabic` with verified OpenType GSUB joining (`مـ` initial, `ـضـ` medial, `ـيـ` medial, `ـا` final, `ف` isolated) and GPOS mark-to-base dot anchoring.
- **Latin (`MIDYAF`)**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Weight 600 SemiBold, tracked `+0.38em`) — **SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1)**, instantiated directly from `@fontsource-variable/plus-jakarta-sans`.
