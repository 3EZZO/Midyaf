# MIDYAF (مضياف) — Brand Identity Proposal (Lux · R2)

## Concept Rationale
**One idea:** *An open-courtyard architectural M.* Two mirrored 45° reed-pen (*qalam*) wings and inward lower courtyard thresholds on a strict `7u × 6u` modular square grid (`1u` walls, `1u` diagonals, `1u` sills, `1u` aligned central doorway) enclose an architectural reception hall with an unmistakable open entrance (*«باب المضياف مفتوح»*) — 100% flat, ownable, and razor-sharp at `16px`.

## Fonts, Licences & Custom Modifications
All wordmarks in the SVG masters are outlined to pure `<path>` geometry (zero `<text>`, zero embedded fonts or rasters):
- **Arabic (`مضياف`)**: Modified from **Noto Kufi Arabic 700** (**SIL Open Font License 1.1**, `@fontsource/noto-kufi-arabic`), preserving exact OpenType GSUB joining (`مـ` initial, `ـضـ` medial, `ـيـ` medial, `ـا` final, `ف` isolated) and GPOS anchoring, customized with a pronounced **45° *qalam*-bevelled Alef (`ا`) crown**, bold **architectural squared Kufic *nuqat*** (dots), and tightened optical **`ا–ف` kerning**.
- **Latin (`MIDYAF`)**: Modified from **Plus Jakarta Sans 600** (**SIL Open Font License 1.1**, `@fontsource-variable/plus-jakarta-sans`), customized with **45° chamfered `M` shoulders** echoing the emblem and hand-tuned optical pair kerning (`Y·A`, `D·Y`, `A·F`).
