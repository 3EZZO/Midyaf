# Midyaf Investor Pitch: The GTS 2027 Live Demo Script

**Presenter:** Founder of Midyaf  
**Audience:** Investors (Seed Round)  
**Duration:** 10 Minutes  
**Objective:** To prove Midyaf's anti-corruption capabilities and showcase the seamless end-to-end operational flow.

---

## 🛑 Phase 1: The Anti-Corruption Vault (Minutes 0-3)
*The most critical part of the demo to show enterprise value.*

**[Action]** Open the Organizer (Sila) Dashboard. Navigate to "Vendor Quotations" for the *GTS 2027* event.
**[Talking Point]**
> *"Investors, one of the biggest leaks in event profitability is quotation corruption. Employees often leak competitor prices to their friends in hotel or car rental companies. In Midyaf, we have solved this with the 'Triple-Key Vault'."*

**[Action]** Point to the locked quotes on the screen (`[ENCRYPTED - AWAITING UNLOCK]`).
**[Action]** Click **"Initiate Vault Unlock"**.
**[Action]** Bring up two smartphones (simulating Sila Ops and Sila Finance) and a tablet (simulating Midyaf Auditor).
**[Talking Point]**
> *"To open these quotes, three people must authenticate simultaneously. Watch."*

**[Action]** Use FaceID/TouchID on all three devices. Watch the dashboard avatars turn green one by one.
**[Result]** The UI flashes, and the quotes unmask instantly.
**[Talking Point]**
> *"The vault is unlocked. An immutable audit log is generated. Corruption is mathematically impossible. We will select the Ritz-Carlton and Royal Fleet."*

## 🎫 Phase 2: VIP Onboarding & Smart Invitations (Minutes 3-5)
**[Action]** Navigate to the "Guest List" tab. Click **"Generate Invitations"**.
**[Action]** Open WhatsApp on a demo phone simulating VIP Guest "John Doe".
**[Talking Point]**
> *"Now that our vendors are secured, we import our VIPs. Midyaf instantly sends them a personalized, royal-grade WhatsApp invitation."*

**[Action]** Open the Smart QR Pass link from the WhatsApp message.
**[Talking Point]**
> *"This dynamic QR pass contains their flight details, their assigned driver (Ahmed), and their hotel booking. No more messy PDF tickets or lost emails."*

**[Action]** Open the Midyaf Captain App on another device. Click "Arrived at RUH Airport".

**[Talking Point]**
